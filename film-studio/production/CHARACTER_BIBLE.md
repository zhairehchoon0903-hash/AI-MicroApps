# THE LAST BLOODLINE — Character Bible

**Status key:** CONFIRMED / PROPOSED / OPEN / REJECTED.  
This file is intentionally conservative. It records what is known and leaves unapproved identity details blank rather than inventing canon.

## 1. Auren — central protagonist

### Confirmed foundation
- A baby is taken from his original world.
- A Fairy intervenes to help him; the intervention goes wrong.
- He is transferred into the human world.
- He grows up as an orphan and is bullied.
- He displays extraordinary powers even as a baby.
- His growth and evolution are central to the series.
- The Five Kingdoms and their factions seek him for their own reasons.
- **Combat identity:** Primarily a swordsman with extraordinary magical powers. Swordsmanship remains his core discipline; magic enhances and evolves his sword techniques rather than replacing the sword.

### Name
- **Auren Vale:** OPEN for final creator confirmation in this production bible. The protagonist is referred to as Auren in planning because that name has been used in the project discussions; do not treat the surname as confirmed canon until approved.

### Personality arc — development framework, not locked biography
Track Auren's progression across these dimensions:
- Sense of belonging: from uncertainty and isolation toward a chosen understanding of home and identity.
- Relationship with power: from instinctive or uncontrolled manifestations toward knowledge, discipline, and responsibility.
- Trust: from vulnerability and guardedness toward earned bonds and discernment.
- Courage: from survival responses toward deliberate moral courage.
- Combat: from no formal sword training as a baby toward a distinct, earned swordsman identity.

Do not assume he is naturally arrogant, cruel, emotionless, or destined to become a villain. Let his choices establish his character.

### Life stages to design
| Stage | Story function | Still to approve |
|---|---|---|
| Baby | Abduction, intervention, first extraordinary manifestations, transfer | Exact age, appearance, powers, clothing, keepsakes |
| Early childhood | Orphanhood, first friendships or isolation, bullying, unexplained incidents | Age range, home, caretakers, location, key incidents |
| Later childhood | Increasing awareness that he is different | Training, mentor, first controlled ability |
| Adolescence | Identity conflict, stronger abilities, pressure from outside forces | Appearance, relationships, first major battle |
| Young adult | Skilled swordsman whose magic has evolved with his discipline | Final design, weapon, fighting style, role in wider conflict |

### Visual identity — OPEN
Do not generate a definitive model sheet until the creator approves:
- Face shape and defining features
- Eye colour and any magical visual change
- Hair colour, texture, length, and style by age
- Skin tone and other non-sensitive visual traits
- Height and build by age
- Clothing by life stage and social setting
- Distinctive marks, accessories, or keepsakes
- Sword design, scabbard, grip, materials, and history

Once approved, create a front / three-quarter / profile reference, expression sheet, full-body turnaround, and age-stage references. Keep the same identity across all generated shots.

### Power record — OPEN
For every ability, record: name, first appearance, trigger, visible effect, range, duration, limits, cost, failure mode, training required, and story consequence. Do not invent an unlimited power set. Baby manifestations should be extraordinary but not automatically controlled.

### Sword record — OPEN
The sword's name, origin, design, material, maker, inheritance, and magical properties are undecided. Do not assume it is an heirloom or sentient weapon.

## 2. The Fairy who intervenes

### Confirmed
A Fairy intervenes to help the abducted baby. Something goes wrong during the intervention, and Auren is transferred into the human world.

### Open decisions
- Name, age, appearance, personality, rank, abilities, and personal history
- Relationship to the baby before the intervention
- Why the Fairy chooses to help
- What the Fairy knows about the abduction
- What specifically goes wrong
- Whether the Fairy survives, is separated, is captured, or remains in the original world
- Whether the Fairy can later find Auren

Do not assume the Fairy is Auren's parent, guardian, relative, or lifelong companion unless the creator establishes it.

## 3. Human-world figures — OPEN

Auren grows up as an orphan. The identity and circumstances of the people who raise, shelter, neglect, exploit, befriend, or bully him have not been finalized.

Create a separate profile for each approved figure:
- Name and age at first appearance
- Relationship to Auren
- Public face versus private motive
- What they want in the scene
- What they fear losing
- Their history with Auren
- Voice, mannerisms, and visual reference
- Their change across the story
- What they know about Auren's origin, and when they learn it

Do not assume names such as Daniel or Maya Vale are canon.

## 4. Kingdoms, factions, and supporting cast — OPEN

Every recurring leader, warrior, scholar, ruler, agent, ally, rival, and antagonist needs an individual profile. Their motives for seeking Auren must be distinct and internally coherent.

Avoid making every opponent evil for the same reason. Some may fear him, some may want to protect him, some may seek political advantage, some may want knowledge, and some may believe they are preventing catastrophe. These are possible motive categories, not assigned canon.

## 5. Character continuity checklist

Before approving any generated shot, verify:
- Same face and age stage
- Same hair, eye appearance, body proportions, and distinctive marks
- Correct costume for the scene and timeline
- Correct props and which hand holds them
- Correct injuries, dirt, wetness, and fatigue
- Correct emotional state and performance
- Correct power manifestation for the character's current development stage
- Correct weapon design and handling
- No unapproved costume, weapon, or power changes
