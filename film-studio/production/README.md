# THE LAST BLOODLINE — Production Bible

This folder is the canonical creative-control centre for the film project. It is designed to keep writing, storyboarding, image generation, video generation, editing, and future episodes consistent.

## Read in this order

1. [Production Bible](PRODUCTION_BIBLE.md) — project identity, rules, tone, and canon policy.
2. [Character Bible](CHARACTER_BIBLE.md) — Auren and the cast; confirmed traits are separated from decisions still awaiting approval.
3. [World Bible](WORLD_BIBLE.md) — Five Kingdoms, peoples, factions, magic, geography, and political structure without inventing unapproved lore.
4. [Episode 01 Blueprint](EPISODE_01_BLUEPRINT.md) — the opening story sequence from the baby’s abduction to the human world.
5. [Continuity Ledger](CONTINUITY_LEDGER.md) — the production record for visual, story, prop, power, and timeline continuity.

## Canon rule

**Confirmed** means explicitly established by the creator. **Proposed** means a creative option that must not be treated as canon until approved. **Open** means information is intentionally unresolved. The AI Director and all production tools must preserve these distinctions.

## Production status

These documents establish the first production-bible foundation. They do not claim that all character designs, kingdom identities, magic rules, or episode scripts have been finalized. Update the ledger whenever a decision becomes canon.
